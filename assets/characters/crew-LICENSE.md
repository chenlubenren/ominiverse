# Station crew attribution and licenses

This notice applies to `station-crew-male.glb` and `station-crew-female.glb`.
These files combine CC0 human/system assets and an attributed CC-BY garment.
They are **not entirely CC0**. The separate [MIT notice](LICENSE.md) covers the
Rocketbox office characters, not these station crew files.

## Coverall: Elvaerwyn — CC-BY 2.5 Sweden

The workwear is adapted from **elvs_male_coveralls_1**, by **Elvaerwyn**.
The source MHCLO's internal name and OBJ filename are
`elvs_male_mechanics_coverall1a`. Both source file headers state `author: Elvaerwyn`
and `license: CC-BY`.

  https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/suits03/suits03_cc-by.zip

  https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/suits03/suits03_cc-by.zip

  (historical reference; this page returned 404 when checked on 2026-11-17).

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/se/deed.en

- The MakeHuman asset-pack FAQ explicitly links its `CC-BY` designation to that

Modifications in this project: fitted to separate male and female bodies;
converted to an opaque matte white-clay material; refined and optimized topology;
added fitted front closure/zipper, chest and thigh pockets and garment seams;
transferred skin weights to the shared anatomical rig; normalized size and
exported as GLB for the station's floating and working animations. The source
author does not endorse this project.

When redistributing these crew models or derivatives, retain appropriate credit
to Elvaerwyn, the source and license links, and an indication of modifications.
For example:

> Station crew coveralls adapted from “elvs_male_coveralls_1” by Elvaerwyn,
> CC BY 2.5 SE, via MakeHuman Community's suits03 pack. Modified for male/female
> fit, white-clay rendering, garment details, rigging and real-time use.

## Human, rig and system assets: MakeHuman — CC0 1.0

The body meshes, body-shaping targets, built-in rig and system assets originate
from **MakeHuman / MakeHuman Community**, used through MPFB. The selected system
assets are `eyes/high-poly`, `hair/short04`, `hair/short02`, and `clothes/shoes05`.
Their MHCLO headers explicitly state that they were released as CC0 in September
2020 and name the former holders Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.

This distinction does not remove the separate attribution requirement of
Elvaerwyn's third-party coverall used in these composite models.

## Clothing reference photographs

NASA/Bill Stafford photographs were used only to study non-pressurized flight
workwear construction. They are not embedded as textures and the crew models do
not reproduce the photographed astronauts' identities, agency marks or mission
insignia. References and their exact source pages are listed in
[crew-README.md](crew-README.md).
